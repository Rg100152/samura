武士道 · Bushidō

Seven virtues.
One blade.

The samurai did not carry honor in the scabbard — it lived in the space between breath and action. These are the seven pillars that held the warrior upright when the world knelt.

01 義

Gi Rectitude

Decide swiftly and justly. The straight path is not the easy one — it is simply the only one worth walking.

02 勇

Yū Courage

To live when it is right to live. To fall when it is right to fall. Fear is a companion, never a master.

03 仁

Jin Benevolence

Strength without compassion is butchery. The blade is sheathed for the weak as often as it is drawn for the just.

04 礼

Rei Respect

Courtesy is the armor of the soul. The bow is not a submission — it is a declaration of self-possession.

05 誠

Makoto Sincerity

A word once given is a blade drawn. Speak only what you will defend, and defend it without hesitation.

06 名誉

Meiyo Honor

Reputation is a shadow that outlives the man. Guard it as you would guard your own throat.

07 忠義

Chūgi Loyalty

Devotion without condition. The bond to one's lord, one's word and one's path is forged before the world begins.
